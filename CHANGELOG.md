# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Versions below 1.0.0 are alpha and may include breaking changes.

## [0.1.0-alpha] - Unreleased

- Comparison provenance (platform plan section 6.2). Every compared briefing records structured changes against the issues its snapshot selected: new development, changed fact, correction, resolved prediction, unchanged, and unavailable baseline. Each change links the current finding or prediction to the baseline issue and item; earlier prose is never evidence. The update judge can now answer Correction, stored as `Finding.isCorrection`. The briefing text carries one summary line after the opening (for example "Compared with Rebates of November 24: 1 new, 1 changed."), or "no material changes found", so email and audio say the same. New `GET /v1.0/api/briefings/{id}/comparison`; catalog issue reads include a subscriber-safe `comparison`. Account export adds `comparisons.json`.
- Baseline previews: `POST /v1.0/api/topics/{id}/history/preview` and `POST /v1.0/api/editions/{id}/history/preview` show which earlier issues the next run or composition would compare with, the context size against its budget, and truncation, for saved or unsaved settings. Nothing is saved.
- Edition-level comparison: editions gain `historyMode`, `historyCount`, `historySinceUtc`, `historyBriefingId`, and `historyTokenBudget`. Any mode other than Off compares each composed edition with its own earlier issues, chosen once per queued briefing and reused on retry; Off keeps the topics' own research comparisons as before.
- System briefings inside custom editions: `GET`, `POST .../enumerate`, and `PUT /v1.0/api/editions/{id}/publications` add subscribed catalog publications to an edition. Composition adds a "From your system briefings" section with each published issue in the period (title, date, opening paragraph, and a link to the reader) and never starts system research. Subscription and plan are checked again at every composition; a plan without custom research cannot add publications (402 CapReached).
- Downgrade keep order (platform plan section 8.4): `GET` and `PUT /v1.0/api/account/topic-slots` show custom topics against the plan limit and save which topics keep running when the limit shrinks, ahead of time or after. The run gate follows the chosen order, then the oldest topics. Topics past the limit keep their history but do not run; nothing is paused, archived, or deleted by the choice.
- Dashboard and mobile: baseline previews next to topic and edition comparison settings, a collapsible "Compared with" callout in the briefing and system briefing readers, edition comparison settings and a system briefings picker, and a "Topics that keep running" screen under the plan, with a Topics banner when over the limit. Findings show a Correction label. The C#, JavaScript and Python SDKs cover every new route; MCP adds `preview_topic_history`, `preview_edition_history`, `set_edition_history`, `get_briefing_comparison`, `get_edition_publications`, `set_edition_publications`, `get_topic_slots`, and `set_topic_slots`. iOS universal links include `/dashboard/*`, so edition email links open the app.
- Schema migration 41: `briefingdeltas` and `editionpublications` tables; `findings.iscorrection`, `topics.slotpriority`, edition history columns, and edition snapshots in `runhistorysnapshots` (nullable run and topic, new edition and briefing references). REST_API.md and the Postman collection are regenerated.
- Owner-approved mobile build 11 (store version 0.1.0) carries the stale topic banner, the one-tap notification card, and the TopicStale preference to internal TestFlight and Google Play testing. Signed over-the-air updates remain unavailable on the current Expo plan.
- Stale topic reviews. A recurring topic whose real runs found no new finding for `topicLifecycle.staleDays` (7) gets a keep-or-delete prompt by email, push, and a banner on the dashboard (Home, Today, Topics, and the topic) and in the mobile app. Keep holds off the next prompt for `keepDays` (7); Delete removes the topic now; new findings close the review by themselves; without Keep a reminder goes out `reminderDays` (2) before the deadline and the topic is deleted after `graceDays` (14), with a notice. Routes `GET /v1.0/api/topic-reviews` (and `/enumerate`), `GET /{id}`, `POST /{id}/keep`, `POST /{id}/delete`. Schema migration 40 (`topicreviews`, `useractivitydays`); it follows the crawl cache migration 39.
- New notification kind `TopicStale` (email on by default) with its own email category, so every email and phone notification type can be turned off on its own on the web and in the app. When an account registers its first push device without any push choice, push turns on for new findings, ready briefings and editions, failures, plan limits, due predictions, and stale topics (audio ready stays off).

### Changed

- Usage by model is keyed by endpoint and model name, so the same model on two endpoints is reported separately (`key` is `<endpointId>|<model>`; `modelEndpointId` and `model` carry the parts). Clients that treated the key as the model name need to read `model` instead.
- The implementation plan and the benchmark improvements list moved to `archive/` as historical records. The root `OWNER_ACTIONS.md` was removed; `mobile/OWNER_ACTIONS.md` and `docs/HOSTING.md` hold the remaining owner steps.
- The universal link files still answer 404 `NotConfigured` until configured, and the message now names the setting to fill in. `docs/HOSTING.md` documents this, the MCP server, and the load balancer rules.
- Ranking: `Pipeline.RelevanceGateScore` (off by default) stops feedback, novelty, source, and recency from lifting a finding the model scored below it. Under the benchmark stub, 50 empties the off-topic briefing but hides three relevant pages on the live dataset; a paid round decides the default.
- Update detection without embeddings: a reworded follow-up that shares enough title and summary words with an earlier finding (`Pipeline.TextUpdateSimilarity`, default 0.35) is judged by the model as a duplicate, an update, or new.
- Client config reports whether the platform model key is configured, and the dashboard warns when an endpoint relies on a missing one. The preference line in emails says "from 1 rating".

- Free plan: $3 a month, 1 topic, daily schedules, 2 runs a day, 10 audio minutes. Complimentary: $10, 2 topics, daily, 4 runs, 30 audio minutes. Promotional codes last 30 days by default. Unedited stored values move to the new defaults at startup.
- The Unlimited plan no longer sets a schedule floor; the server's scheduler minimum applies.
