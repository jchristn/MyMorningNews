# Harbor City expands heat pump rebates to renters

The Harbor City Energy Office said on Monday that renters can now claim heat pump rebates of up to $3,000 per household. The change takes effect for installations completed after January 15.

Landlords must sign a consent form before a renter can apply for the rebate. The office will publish the form on its website next week.

Officials said the change follows two years of complaints that renters were shut out of the program. About 41 percent of Harbor City households rent their homes.

# State heat pump tax credit doubles for low income households

Low income households in the state can now claim a heat pump tax credit of $4,000, double the previous amount. The credit applies to heat pumps installed after the start of the next tax year.

Households earning less than 80 percent of the area median income qualify for the larger credit. Other households keep the existing $2,000 credit.

The state revenue department said claims will be filed with the normal income tax return. No separate application is needed.

# New licensing rule for heat pump installers takes effect in March

Heat pump installers in Harbor City will need a state refrigerant license starting March 1. Contractors without the license cannot pull permits for new heat pump installations.

The rule is meant to cut refrigerant leaks from poorly installed systems. Inspectors found leaks in one of every eight systems checked last year.

Homeowners can check whether an installer is licensed on the state contractor board website before signing a contract.

# Harbor City adopts a stricter heat pump efficiency standard

The city council voted 7 to 2 to require new heat pumps to meet the "cold climate" efficiency rating. The rule applies to units installed in new homes and major renovations.

Council members said the rating ensures units keep heating when outdoor temperatures drop below freezing. Older units lose much of their capacity in the city's coldest weeks.

Manufacturers have until the end of next year to supply compliant models to local distributors.
