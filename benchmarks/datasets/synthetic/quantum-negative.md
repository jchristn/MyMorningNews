# Quantum Cafe opens on Main Street with a computing theme

The Quantum Cafe opened on Main Street this week with a menu named after famous computer scientists. The owner, a retired engineer, decorated the walls with old circuit boards.

The cafe serves coffee, sandwiches, and a dessert called the error correction cake. It opens at seven every morning.

The owner plans a monthly trivia night about science and technology.

# Harbor City libraries extend weekend hours

All Harbor City library branches will stay open until eight in the evening on Saturdays starting next month. The change follows a survey of 3,000 library users.

The central library will also open on Sunday mornings. Staffing costs are covered by a state grant.

Story time for children moves to Saturday afternoons.

# Road repairs will close two lanes on Harbor Avenue

Crews will close two lanes of Harbor Avenue for six weeks starting Monday to repair the road surface. Traffic will be reduced to one lane in each direction.

The city recommends using Pier Street as a detour. Buses will keep their normal stops.

The repairs cost $2.1 million.

# Students build robots for the Harbor City science fair

More than 200 students entered the Harbor City science fair this year, many with robots and coding projects. The winner built a robot that sorts recycling.

Judges included engineers from local companies and the community college. Prizes include summer internships.

The fair will be held again next spring.

# Ferry schedule changes for the winter season

The Gull Island ferry will run every 45 minutes instead of every 30 minutes from November through March. The last ferry leaves downtown at eleven in the evening.

Holiday service will follow the Sunday schedule. Riders can check departures on the transit website.

The summer schedule returns in April.
